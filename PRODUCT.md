# Product

## Register

product

## Users
- **Kasir (Cashiers)**: Operational staff processing customer orders, adding items/toppings to cart, finalizing checkouts, and printing physical/digital receipts in a fast-paced environment.
- **Admins (Superusers)**: Owners/Managers monitoring financial reports, managing branches, configuring daily stock targets, tracking raw material purchases, and controlling users/menus.

## Product Purpose
To serve as an enterprise-grade, multi-outlet POS system that guarantees operational efficiency and absolute financial integrity. Key features include centralized branch management (with an instant kill-switch), daily stock target boundaries, auto-calculating multi-toppings, dynamic charts for real-time sales/expense analytics, and immutable price snapshots at the time of transaction.

## Brand Personality
- **Voice & Tone**: Professional, clean, efficient, and direct.
- **Visual Identity**: Reliable, culinary-focused (retaining the existing teal/green palette and ShangHaejo brand logo), structured, and utilitarian.
- **Emotional Goals**: Reliability, trust, precision, and ease of use.

## Anti-references
- **Over-decorated UIs**: Avoid large, slow visual transitions, unnecessary background blurs, and decorative glassmorphism.
- **Low Contrast / Tiny Text**: Avoid hard-to-read muted grays for body or labels that cause eye strain or input errors.
- **Fragile Data Deletions**: Never hide or delete transactions; failed/void orders must remain visually evident for auditable integrity.
- **SaaS-Cliché Templates**: Avoid boilerplate SaaS dashboards with tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows on every widget.

## Design Principles
- **Swift Path to Completion**: Limit cashier clicks. Menu items, toppings, and checkout forms should be accessible within a flat hierarchy.
- **Immutable Transparency**: Audit trails, voided states, and price snapshots must be visually unambiguous.
- **Utilitarian Density**: Maximize information display for reports and order lists without causing visual noise or cognitive overload.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **Contrast**: Target WCAG AA color contrast (minimum 4.5:1) for text/icons on background surfaces to support readability in varying kitchen and counter lighting.
- **Motion**: Adhere to `prefers-reduced-motion` settings to prevent motion sickness or visual fatigue.
